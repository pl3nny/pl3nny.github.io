About Me

I am a Software Quality Operations Lead with over 7.5 years of experience scaling safety, root cause analysis triage, and synthetic testing for industry-leading autonomous vehicle pipelines.

My core focus is bridging the gap between software engineering and operational testing. I specialize in architecting automated Python workflows, managing massive simulation datasets (Foretellix/OSC2), and engineering custom metrics that systematically isolate algorithmic regressions and machine learning anomalies.
